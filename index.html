Autonomy Stack for TRAKR

I worked on helping Addverb's quadruped, TRAKR, find its way through unfamiliar environments. Without wheel odometry, the robot needed a reliable way to estimate its motion and build a map as it moved.

I combined visual-inertial SLAM with a LiDAR fallback for spaces where visual features were limited. The frontend used Structure-from-Motion, ORB features, and motion-only bundle adjustment, while the backend combined LiDAR and visual optimization with Bag-of-Words loop closure.

A Hybrid A* planner, Pure Pursuit controller, and decision tree connected the map to the robot's movement so it could plan and follow paths autonomously.

Quadruped Stabilization Module

I worked on keeping the quadruped's body stable as the ground changed or the robot was disturbed. I combined a downward-facing camera with leg contact estimates, using an Extended Kalman Filter to estimate the ground angle and support posture correction.

Haptic Simulator

Fitting Through

Built a perceptive reinforcement learning system for the Unitree G1 using teacher-student distillation, enabling the humanoid to adapt its posture through crouching, knee-walking, and crawling while traversing constrained terrain. The perception pipeline fuses two depth cameras into a gravity-aligned clearance map and marks occluded regions as unknown instead of treating them as free space.

Visual Interactive Navigation for Legged Robots via Whole-Body Control

Developed a long-horizon reinforcement learning system that coordinates all 17 degrees of freedom on Boston Dynamics Spot for whole-body loco-manipulation. The pipeline combines class-conditioned transformer-based region prediction with geometry-driven grasp generation, then transfers the learned behavior from simulation through domain randomization and policy fine-tuning.

Improved Visual-Inertial Odometry in Dynamic Environments

Developed a visual-inertial odometry pipeline that uses IMU motion priors and Lucas-Kanade optical flow to reject motion-inconsistent features before pose estimation. Low-light preprocessing, PnP with RANSAC, and sliding-window graph optimization help maintain a more stable pose estimate in dynamic scenes where ORB-SLAM exhibits drift.

Visual-Inertial Odometry Optical Flow IMU Preintegration PnP + RANSAC G2O
