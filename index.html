Autonomy Stack for TRAKR

I worked on helping Addverb's quadruped, TRAKR, find its way through unfamiliar environments. Without wheel odometry, the robot needed a reliable way to estimate its motion and build a map as it moved.

I combined visual-inertial SLAM with a LiDAR fallback for spaces where visual features were limited. The frontend used Structure-from-Motion, ORB features, and motion-only bundle adjustment, while the backend combined LiDAR and visual optimization with Bag-of-Words loop closure.

A Hybrid A* planner, Pure Pursuit controller, and decision tree connected the map to the robot's movement so it could plan and follow paths autonomously.

Quadruped Stabilization Module

I worked on keeping the quadruped's body stable as the ground changed or the robot was disturbed. I combined a downward-facing camera with leg contact estimates, using an Extended Kalman Filter to estimate the ground angle and support posture correction.

Haptic Simulator

I built a Unity-based VR simulator where users could get used to force feedback before operating a robot remotely over 5G. It rendered forces in real time and let users practice with different virtual materials, shapes, and resistance levels in repeatable conditions.
